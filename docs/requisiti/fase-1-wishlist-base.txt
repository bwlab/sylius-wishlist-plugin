Stage 1 — Basic Wishlist (MVP)
Task Type: Story​
Title: Implement Basic Product Wishlist Functionality


Description
As a customer, I want to save products to a personal wishlist so that I can easily find them
later and add them to my shopping cart when I'm ready to purchase.

The wishlist must be available to both authenticated customers and guests.

Customers should be able to add products to their wishlist directly from the Product Detail
Page (PDP) and Product Listing Page (PLP), view their saved products, remove products,
and add one or multiple wishlist products to their shopping cart.

The wishlist should work independently of the shopping cart. Adding or removing products
from the wishlist must not automatically affect the cart.


Gherkin Scenarios
Scenario 1 — Add a product to the wishlist from PDP

  None‎
  Given I am browsing a product detail page
  When I click the "Add to Wishlist" button
  Then the product should be added to my wishlist
  And I should see a confirmation that the product was added


Scenario 2 — Add a product to the wishlist from PLP

  None‎
  Given I am browsing a product listing page
  When I click the "Add to Wishlist" button for a product
  Then the selected product should be added to my wishlist
  And I should see a confirmation that the product was added


Scenario 3 — Prevent duplicate wishlist entries

  None‎
  Given a product is already in my wishlist
 When I attempt to add the same product again
 Then the product should not be duplicated
 And my wishlist should contain only one entry for that product


Scenario 4 — Guest customer wishlist

 None‎
 Given I am browsing the store as a guest
 When I add a product to my wishlist
 Then the product should be saved in my guest wishlist
 And I should be able to access it while browsing the store
 And my wishlist should remain available after refreshing the page


Scenario 5 — Authenticated customer wishlist

 None‎
 Given I am logged in as a customer
 And I have added products to my wishlist
 When I log out and log in again
 Then my previously saved wishlist products should still be
 available


Scenario 6 — View wishlist contents

 None‎
 Given I have added multiple products to my wishlist
 When I navigate to my wishlist page
 Then I should see all products saved in my wishlist
 And each product should display its name, image, and current
 price
 And each product should have an option to remove it
 And each product should have an option to add it to my cart


Scenario 7 — Display an empty wishlist

 None‎
 Given my wishlist does not contain any products
 When I navigate to my wishlist page
 Then I should see an appropriate empty wishlist message
 And I should be able to continue shopping


Scenario 8 — Remove a product from the wishlist

 None‎
 Given my wishlist contains multiple products
 When I remove one of the products
 Then the selected product should disappear from my wishlist
 And all other wishlist products should remain unchanged


Scenario 9 — Add a single wishlist product to the cart

 None‎
 Given my wishlist contains a product available for purchase
 When I click "Add to Cart" for that product
 Then the selected product should be added to my shopping cart
 And the product should remain in my wishlist
 And the shopping cart should reflect the newly added product


Scenario 10 — Add multiple wishlist products to the cart

 None‎
 Given my wishlist contains multiple products available for
 purchase
 And I have selected two or more products
 When I click "Add Selected to Cart"
 Then all selected products should be added to my shopping cart
 And products that were not selected should not be added
 And all products should remain in my wishlist


Scenario 11 — Handle a product unavailable for purchase

 None‎
 Given my wishlist contains a product that is no longer available
 for purchase
 When I view my wishlist
 Then the product should remain visible in my wishlist
 And I should not be able to add the unavailable product to my
 cart
  And I should see an indication that the product is unavailable



User Acceptance Criteria
   ●​   Customers can add products to their wishlist from both PDP and PLP.
   ●​   Wishlist functionality is available to authenticated customers and guests.
   ●​   Guest wishlists survive page refreshes within the same browser.
   ●​   Authenticated customer wishlists persist between login sessions.
   ●​   Adding the same product multiple times does not create duplicate entries.
   ●​   Customers can access a dedicated wishlist page.
   ●​   The wishlist page displays each product's name, image, and current price.
   ●​   Customers can remove individual products from their wishlist.
   ●​   An empty wishlist displays an appropriate message.
   ●​   Customers can add a single product from their wishlist to their cart.
   ●​   Customers can select multiple wishlist products and add them to their cart in one
        action.
   ●​   Adding products to the cart does not automatically remove them from the wishlist.
   ●​   Products unavailable for purchase remain visible but cannot be added to the cart.
   ●​   Wishlist operations do not unexpectedly modify existing cart contents.
   ●​   The complete functionality can be demonstrated through the storefront UI.


Technical Details
The implementation is technology-independent. Participants are free to choose their
architecture, persistence strategy, and UI approach.

The following are suggestions rather than mandatory requirements.

Guest wishlist persistence

Consider using cookies or browser storage to identify or maintain a guest wishlist. A
server-side wishlist associated with an anonymous identifier is also acceptable.

Authenticated wishlist persistence

Consider storing wishlists in persistent storage and associating them with customer
accounts.

Product and variant handling

Both Sylius and PrestaShop support product variants or combinations. Participants should
use a consistent approach when deciding whether wishlist entries represent products or
specific purchasable variants.
Cart integration

Use the platform's existing cart functionality rather than implementing a separate shopping
cart.

Existing cart validation rules, including product availability, should remain respected.

Frontend implementation

AJAX, asynchronous updates, and dynamic wishlist counters are welcome but not required.
Traditional form submissions and page reloads are acceptable.

Testing

Automated tests are welcome but not required for the hackathon.

The functionality will be evaluated through a live demonstration against the acceptance
criteria.
